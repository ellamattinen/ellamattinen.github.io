As a hobby, I like to make crosswords (or arrowword puzzles) from scratch. As I come up with the words as well as the hints, they may be more difficult for some, although most words are common. These puzzles are mostly in English, but I make some in Finnish as well.

Below are some basic rules/hints to help you get started:

  • One letter per cell and words are usually written either from left to right or from up to down unless otherwise specified using an arrow.
  • Stars indicate relation to the bigger image (can be any word relating to it).
  • Some answers may not be words, but abbreviations or names etc.
  • A cell with a hint usually includes a hint for both the word on the right as well as a word for below unless there is no space for both.
  • Big arrows on hints indicate where the word goes and in which direction if not clear otherwise.
